Emplio Cloud has three monthly plans, a 7-day free trial, and top-ups for busy months. Prices are on the [pricing page](https://emplio.app/pricing).

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly usage |
|---|---|
| **Starter** | The base amount |
| **Growth** | 2.5 times Starter |
| **Pro** | 5 times Starter |

Usage covers everything Emplio does for you: chats, background work, its computer and its browser.

## Free trial

- A new business gets 7 days free, with no card needed.
- It runs on the Standard AI and covers a few small jobs. If that runs out first, Emplio pauses until you pick a plan.
- One trial per person.

## Top-ups

If you run out before the month ends, buy a top-up of an extra half, 1 or 2 months of usage. A top-up lasts 12 months.
