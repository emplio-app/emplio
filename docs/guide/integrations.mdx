Connect your apps and Emplio works in them for you: reads your mail, sends invoices, keeps the calendar. Your connections are yours: only your chats use them.

## Connect an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Press your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the app">
    On **Browse**, search or pick a category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Connect">
    Sign in to the app in the small window that opens. Some apps ask you to paste a key instead. It's stored encrypted and Emplio never sees it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On **My connections** you can rename, **Reconnect** or **Disconnect** an account. Use **Add another** for a second account, like work and personal Gmail.

## Approvals

Looking things up never asks. Before Emplio sends, edits or deletes anything in an app, it asks you in the chat: **Allow**, **Always allow** or **Deny**. To undo an **Always allow**, remove it under **Always allowed** in [Settings](/guide/settings).

## MCP servers

The **MCP** tab adds tools from other services by their MCP address. The whole team can use them. Only owners and admins can add them.
