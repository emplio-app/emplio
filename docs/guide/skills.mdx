A skill is a saved how-to: the steps for a job, done the way your business does it. Emplio makes and improves skills as it works.

## Show Emplio how

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the live browser">
    Ask Emplio to open the website, then press **Live browser**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Show Emplio how">
    Pick **Record voice** to talk it through, or **Clicks only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the task once">
    Click and type as you normally would. Passwords aren't recorded. What you save really saves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Done">
    Emplio turns the recording into a skill and tells you in a line what it can now do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A recording can run up to 30 minutes. Press **Cancel** to throw it away.

## Use a skill

Type `/` at the start of a message and pick a skill. Skills marked **yours** are your own; the rest are the business's. Emplio also picks the right skill by itself.

## Whose skills

| Made in | Saved for |
|---|---|
| An owner's or admin's chat | The whole business, or that person |
| A staff chat | That person. Changes to business skills go to the owner as a proposal. |

## Skills from the skill store

Emplio can also find a ready-made skill in the public [skill store](https://skills.sh). It reads the skill and its scripts first, to check it fits the job, before asking you. Every install shows a card with what it is, where it's from and what the store's security checks found. Nothing installs until you press **Allow**.
